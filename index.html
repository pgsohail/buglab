What kind of projects do you take on?

Blockchain and Web3 builds, AI and ML products, data pipelines and analytics, accounting automation, plus QA and debugging on existing codebases. If it's software and it needs to work properly, we're interested.

Do you work with startups or enterprises?

Both. We bring enterprise-grade engineering to startups and growing businesses, and have delivered for clients across the USA, UK, UAE and beyond.

Can you fix or take over an existing codebase?

Yes. Bug detection, refactoring and testing are core to what we do. We'll audit what you have and tell you honestly what to keep.
